# AutoHoldClick
A simple python app built with tkinter that holds the left click pressed. Simple activation by pressing the middle click down, also with audio confirmation. The app works on both Windows and macOs.
## Usage
Activate the click holder by just pressing left click. You can stop it by clicking again.
## Permissions
**Windows** - You are good to go out of the box.<br />
**MacOS** - You will need to give the app permissions for **Input Monitoring** and **Accesibility**.
